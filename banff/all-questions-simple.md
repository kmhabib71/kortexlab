## How to Use These Questions

**DON'T:**
- Read these like a script
- Ask all 45+ questions
- Make it feel like an interrogation

**DO:**
- Use these as prep/backup
- Let conversation flow naturally
- Ask follow-ups based on their answers
- Listen 80%, talk 20%
- Focus on "walk me through..." and "show me..." questions

**Questions will naturally get answered when you ask:**
- "Walk me through your typical week"
- "Show me how you do [specific task]"
- "What takes the most time?"
- "Where do things break?"

**You'll probably ask directly: 10-15 questions**
**You'll get answers to: 30-35 questions through natural conversation**
