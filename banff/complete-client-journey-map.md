# Complete Executive Client Journey - Banff Advisors
## From First Contact to Ongoing Management

---

## 🎯 Overview: The Full Journey

```
FIRST CONTACT → SALES → CONTRACT → ONBOARDING → MONTH 1-2 (Standardized) → MONTH 3+ (Ongoing)
   (Days)      (Weeks)   (Hours)    (60 min)         (8 weeks)              (Years)
```

---

# PHASE 1: FIRST CONTACT (Day 0)

## How Potential Clients Find Banff

### Channel 1: LinkedIn DM (Most Common) 📱
**What happens:**
- Someone DMs David/Liv/Claire on LinkedIn
- Example: "Hi David, saw your post about exec placement. I'm a CFO looking for my next role."

**Systems used:**
- ❌ **NOT tracked anywhere** (this is a problem!)
- Just lives in advisor's LinkedIn messages

---

### Channel 2: Email (Common) 📧
**What happens:**
- Someone emails advisor directly
- Example: "john@example.com emails david@banffadvisors.com"

**Systems used:**
- ✅ **Gmail** - Email arrives
- ✅ **HubSpot** - Auto-logs email (because HubSpot tracks all Gmail activity)
- ❌ But advisor doesn't create "deal" in HubSpot (should but doesn't)

---

### Channel 3: Referral (Common) 🤝
**What happens:**
- Existing client says: "You should talk to my friend Sarah, she's a great CMO"
- Advisor reaches out: "Hi Sarah, John Smith suggested we connect"

**Systems used:**
- ❌ **NOT tracked** - Referral source not logged
- Just email or LinkedIn message

---

### Channel 4: Website Form (Rare, Ignored) 🌐
**What happens:**
- Someone fills form on banffadvisors.com
- Form dumps to **Slack channel**
- David: "We do a piss poor job" handling these

**Systems used:**
- ✅ **Website form** → **Slack** (automated)
- ❌ **No follow-up system** - often ignored

# PHASE 2: SALES PROCESS (Week 1-4)

## Step 1: Initial Sales Call 📞

**Who's involved:**
- Advisor (David, Liv, or Claire)
- Potential client (exec)

**What happens:**
- 30-60 min Zoom call
- Advisor asks about:
  - Current situation ("Just left my CFO role at TechCo")
  - What they're looking for ("Board roles in fintech")
  - Timeline ("Not urgent, but exploring")

**Systems used:**
- ✅ **Zoom** - Video call
- ⚠️ **Notion** - Advisor manually writes notes AFTER call
  - File location: Advisor's personal notes (unstructured)
  - Not tied to any CRM record
  - Just freeform notes like: "Talked to Sarah - CFO, fintech, board-focused"

**What's BROKEN:**
- ❌ No HubSpot deal created
- ❌ No follow-up reminder set
- ❌ No pipeline tracking
- ❌ Advisor just "remembers" to follow up (or forgets)

---

## Step 2: Send Pitch Deck 📄

**What happens:**
- Advisor sends email with:
  - Banff pitch deck (PDF)
  - Pricing information
  - "Let me know if you want to move forward"

**Systems used:**
- ✅ **HubSpot template email** (pre-written, advisor clicks send)
- ✅ **Gmail** sends the email
- ✅ **HubSpot** auto-logs that email was sent

**What's BROKEN:**
- ❌ No tracking if client opened the email
- ❌ No reminder to follow up in 1 week
- ❌ If client doesn't reply, advisor just... hopes they remember to check back

---

## Step 3: Follow-Up (Maybe?) 🤷

**What SHOULD happen:**
- Week 1: Send pitch deck
- Week 2: Follow up if no response
- Week 3: Second follow up
- Week 4: Move to "not interested" or keep nurturing

**What ACTUALLY happens:**
- Advisor sends pitch deck
- ...waits for client to reply
- If no reply, advisor probably forgets
- **Deals are lost here** because no follow-up

**Systems used:**
- ❌ **NOTHING** - No automated reminders
- ❌ No pipeline to track "sent deck, waiting for response"

---

## Step 4: Client Says YES! 🎉

**What happens:**
- Client replies: "Yes, I want to sign up!"
- Advisor says: "Great! I'll send you the contract"

**Systems used:**
- ❌ **Email only** - No system updated yet

## WEEK 1: KICKOFF CALL 🎤

**What happens:**
- Sarah receives Typeform link in welcome email

**Questions on form:**
```
- What's your current career situation?
- What are you looking for? (new role, board seats, etc.)
- Target companies/industries?
- Geographic preferences?
- Timeline? (urgent, 3-6 months, exploring)
- Career highlights?
- Notable achievements?
- etc.
```

**Systems used:**
- ✅ **Typeform** - Form responses
- ⚠️ Responses auto-save to Typeform, Max reviews before call

---

### The Kickoff Call (60-90 minutes)

**Who's on the call:**
- Sarah (client)
- Max (client ops owner)
- David (advisor)

**What happens:**
- Full career deep-dive:
  - "Walk me through your career from the beginning"
  - "What are you most proud of?"
  - "What roles are you targeting?"
  - "What's your ideal next step?"
  - "Who's in your network?"
  - "What's your timeline?"

**Systems used during call:**
- ✅ **Zoom** - Video call
- ✅ **Zoom AI** - Auto-recording + transcription

**Systems used after call:**

### Automatic: Zoom Transcript Dumps to Notion
- **Zapier** (integration tool) runs automation:
  - Trigger: Zoom recording finished
  - Action: Create page in Notion database "Kickoff Full Transcripts"

**What gets created automatically:**
```
Notion Page Title: "Banff Advisor Kickoff Full Transcript - [Date]"
Content: Full word-for-word transcript from Zoom AI
Attendees: Max Queller, David, Sarah Johnson (detected from Zoom)
Date: Nov 25, 2025
Duration: 78 minutes
```

**What's GOOD:** Transcript saved automatically ✅

**What's BROKEN:**
- ❌ Transcript is NOT tagged to Sarah's client page (manual step needed)
- ❌ Call type is NOT tagged as "Kickoff" (manual step needed)
- ❌ Action items are NOT extracted (buried in transcript)

---

### Manual: Max Tags the Call (30 minutes)

**After the call, Max has to:**

1. **Find the transcript in Notion**
   - Go to "Kickoff Full Transcripts" database
   - Find the most recent one (hopefully Sarah's, but might be mixed with other calls)

2. **Tag to Sarah's client page**
   - Open transcript page
   - Add relation: "Client" → Sarah Johnson
   - Now transcript appears on Sarah's page

3. **Tag call type**
   - Field: "Call Type" → Select "Kickoff"
   - This updates the Retool dashboard (shows Sarah had kickoff)

4. **Extract action items manually**
   - Read through transcript (or remember from call)
   - Write separate "Kickoff Notes" document with:
     - Key takeaways
     - Career goals
     - Target companies
     - Action items: "Update resume", "Build LinkedIn profile", "Start trending campaign"

5. **Update Contender profile**
   - Go to **Contender** → Sarah's profile
   - Add "Kickoff Date" field
   - Update any tags based on what was learned in call
     - Maybe add more industry tags
     - Update target companies

6. **Update HubSpot**
   - Add "Kickoff Complete" date field

**Systems used:**
- ✅ **Notion** - Tagging transcript, creating notes
- ✅ **Contender** - Update profile
- ✅ **HubSpot** - Mark milestone complete

**Time:** 30-45 minutes

**What's BROKEN:**
- ❌ All manual work
- ❌ Action items not automatically extracted
- ❌ Easy to miss tagging (then dashboard breaks)

## WEEK 4-8: TRENDING CAMPAIGN (2 months) 📊

**What "Trending" means:**
Send Sarah's profile to 50+ PE/VC firms over 8 weeks to generate opportunities.

---

### Step 1: Profile Freshness Check (15 min)

**What Max does BEFORE trending:**

**The embarrassment-prevention step:**
1. Open **Sarah's LinkedIn**
2. Open **Sarah's Contender profile**
3. Compare side-by-side:
   - Current job title match?
   - Current company match?
   - Recent experience match?

**If LinkedIn updated but Contender is stale:**
- ⚠️ **STOP! Don't send trending yet!**
- Update Contender profile first (10-15 min)
- Example: Sarah got a new job but Contender shows old role → looks bad

**Why this matters:**
- David: "if we don't do it well, **we look like idiots**"
- Sending outdated profiles to PE/VC partners is embarrassing
- Damages Banff's reputation

**Systems used:**
- ✅ **LinkedIn** - Check current profile
- ✅ **Contender** - Update if needed

**What's BROKEN:**
- ❌ All manual comparison (should be automated)
- ❌ No alerts when LinkedIn changes
- ❌ Easy to miss updates

---

### Step 2: Select Target B4B Partners (30 min)

**What Max does:**

Opens **Contender** → B4B Partners (database of PE/VC firms)

**Filters by:**
- Industry focus: Fintech, SaaS (matches Sarah's targets)
- Role needs: CFO, Finance exec (matches Sarah's function)
- Geography: Remote-friendly, Bay Area (matches Sarah's prefs)
- Firm size: Mid-market PE, Growth equity

**Result:** List of ~50 firms that might be interested in Sarah

**Manual selection:**
- Review each firm

**Systems used:**
- ✅ **Contender** - Partner database & filtering

**What's BROKEN:**
- ❌ No tracking of "already sent to this partner"
- ❌ Risk of sending same profile twice (annoying for partner)
- ❌ All manual selection (should be smart-filtered)

- Give time for responses

**Systems used:**
- ✅ **Contender** - "Whisper" sending feature

**What's BROKEN:**
- ❌ Manual sends each week (should be scheduled automatically)
- ❌ No tracking of which partners received which profiles (some tracking in Contender, but limited)

## Key Insights

### ✅ What Works:
1. **Month 1-2 standardized process** - Everyone does same steps

3. **Zapier** - Zoom → Notion transcripts

### ❌ What's Broken:
1. **No sales pipeline** - Deals lost from lack of follow-up
2. **4-system onboarding** - 60 min of duplicate data entry
3. **Contender has no API** - All manual clicking
4. **Ad-hoc task chaos** - Email/Slack/Zoom fragmentation

6. **Profile staleness** - Risk of embarrassment
7. **Manual Retool updates** - Human error breaks dashboard

### 🎯 Biggest Time Sucks:
1. **Task extraction** - 10-20 hrs/week finding tasks in email/Slack/Zoom
2. **Client onboarding** - 60 min per client across 4 systems
3. **Trending campaigns** - 3-4 hrs per campaign (manual sends, no tracking)
4. **P2P coordination** - 30 min each, no tracking, wasted intros
