## 🎯 Overview: B4B vs Executive Side

**Key Difference:**

| Aspect | Executive Side | B4B (Enterprise) Side |
|--------|---------------|----------------------|
| **Who are clients?** | Individual executives (CEOs, CFOs) | PE firms, VC firms, search firms |
| **What they want** | Job opportunities, career guidance | Access to executive talent |
| **What they pay for** | Advisory services ($X,XXX/month) | Access to executive database + placement fees |
| **Primary system** | Notion (client ops hub) | Contender (profile sharing platform) |
| **Who manages** | Max, Lily (Client Ops) | Kayla, Amanda (B4B Ops) |
| **Volume** | 212 active exec clients | ~50-100 B4B partners (estimated) |

---

## 🏢 What is B4B (Banff for Business)?

**B4B clients are the "buyers" in Banff's marketplace.**

### Types of B4B Clients:

1. **Private Equity (PE) Firms**
   - Example: Vista Equity Partners, Thoma Bravo
   - Need: Executives for portfolio companies (CEOs, CFOs, CTOs)
   - Use case: "We just bought a SaaS company, need a new CEO"

2. **Venture Capital (VC) Firms**
   - Example: Andreessen Horowitz, Sequoia Capital
   - Need: Executives for portfolio companies, board members
   - Use case: "Our Series B company needs a CFO"

3. **Executive Search Firms (Less Common)**
   - Example: Korn Ferry, Spencer Stuart
   - Need: Access to executive candidates
   - Use case: "Client looking for healthcare CEO, any matches?"

# ❌ B4B Pain Points Summary

## High-Impact Pain Points:

### 1. Monthly Pipeline Review (4-5 hrs/month waste)
- **Problem:** Manual Zoom call to review every deal
- **Process:** Chat → Notion notes → Email action items → Manual follow-up
- **Fix:** Automated reminders, task assignment in HubSpot

### 2. No Whisper/Intro Tracking
- **Problem:** Can't track which execs sent to which firms
- **Risk:** Send same profile twice (annoying)
- **Fix:** Contender needs tracking table: "Whisper History"

### 3. No Outcome Tracking
- **Problem:** Don't know which whispers → intros → hires
- **Impact:** Can't measure ROI, can't improve matching
- **Fix:** Track full funnel: Whisper → Response → Intro → Interview → Hire

### 4. Manual Intro Coordination
- **Problem:** Every intro is manual email back-and-forth
- **Process:** B4B requests → Kayla searches → Advisor approves → Email exec → Facilitate intro
- **Fix:** Automated intro request workflow

### 5. Approval Bottleneck
- **Problem:** Kayla waits for advisor to approve exec matches
- **Time lost:** 1-2 days per search
- **Fix:** Pre-approved criteria, advisor only reviews finals

### 6. No Engagement Tracking
- **Problem:** Don't know if B4B partners VIEW profiles sent
- **Impact:** Wasting whispers on inactive partners
- **Fix:** Contender needs view tracking (like LinkedIn "who viewed your profile")

### 7. Profile Staleness (Same as Exec Side)
- **Problem:** Risk of sending outdated exec profiles to B4B firms
- **Impact:** Embarrassment, damages credibility
- **Fix:** LinkedIn monitoring, freshness alerts
