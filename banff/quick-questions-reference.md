## General Approach (80% Listen, 20% Talk)

- Let them explain processes first
- Ask "walk me through..." questions
- Ask "what happens when..." for edge cases
- Ask "where does this live?" for every workflow
- Ask "who else touches this?" to map handoffs
- Take notes on:
  - Time spent (hours per week/month)
  - Tools used
  - Where things break
  - Manual steps they hate
