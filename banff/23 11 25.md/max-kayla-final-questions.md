## SECTION 1: THE PAIN (15 minutes)

### Goal: Understand what HURTS the most

---

### Q1. **"Walk me through your Friday last week - what took the most time?"**

**Why ask this:**
- Real example vs theoretical
- Shows actual time distribution
- Reveals hidden pain points

**Listen for:**
- Time spent on each activity
- Interruptions/context switching
- Things that "shouldn't take this long"

**Follow-up if needed:**
- "How much of that was waiting on someone else?"
- "Which parts felt like repetitive clicking?"

---

### Q2. **"If I could give you back 10 hours this week, which task would you eliminate first?"**

**Why ask this:**
- Forces prioritization
- Reveals #1 pain point
- Shows what they value most

**Listen for:**
- Emotional response (frustration = high priority)
- Specific task names
- "I would love to never do X again"

**For Max:** Probably client onboarding or task hunting
**For Kayla:** Probably monthly pipeline reviews or intro coordination

---

### Q3. **"What breaks most often? What makes you say 'oh crap, not again'?"**

**Why ask this:**
- Reveals brittle processes
- Shows where errors happen
- Uncovers hidden time sinks

**Listen for:**
- Retool dashboard issues (missed tagging)
- Duplicate work (sent same profile twice)
- Data sync failures (Whale Sync issues)

## SECTION 2: CLIENT ONBOARDING (Max-focused, 15 minutes)

### Goal: Get details on the 60-minute manual process

**Say this to Kayla:**
> "Kayla, I'll focus on Max for client onboarding since he does this daily, but jump in if you see similar issues on B4B side."

---

### Q4. **"Max, can you screen-share and show me the last client you onboarded?"**

**Why ask this:**
- SEE the actual process (not described)
- Identify UI bottlenecks
- Spot hidden steps they forgot to mention

**What to observe:**
- How many tabs open
- Copy-paste between systems
- Manual typing vs dropdowns
- Time spent searching/hunting

**Ask during screen-share:**
- "What are you waiting for here?" (loading times)
- "Do you have to look this up somewhere else?" (context switching)
- "How often do you get this wrong and have to fix it?" (error rate)

---

### Q5. **"What's the most annoying part of Contender setup? Is it the photo, the tags, or something else?"**

**Why ask this:**
- Validates transcript finding (photo download is pain)
- Might reveal worse pain we don't know about
- Shows specific UI frustrations

**Listen for:**
- "The worst part is..." (priority)
- "I wish Contender could..." (feature gap)
- "Sometimes I just skip..." (workarounds)

---

### Q6. **"When you get a signed contract PDF from TSQ, what info is missing that you have to hunt for?"**

**Why ask this:**
- Reveals data handoff gaps
- Shows where automation can pre-fill
- Uncovers communication issues

**Listen for:**
- LinkedIn URL (often missing)
- Billing details (sometimes unclear)
- Client background (have to ask advisor)

## SECTION 3: TASK CHAOS (Both Max + Kayla, 10 minutes)

### Goal: Understand the email/Slack/Zoom fragmentation

---

### Q7. **"Show me your inbox right now - what's a typical 'task email' from David look like?"**

**Why ask this:**
- See actual format (not described)
- Understand how tasks arrive
- Spot patterns AI can detect

**What to observe:**
- Email structure (bullet list? paragraphs? vague?)
- How many tasks per email (1 or 10?)
- Urgency indicated (or guessed?)

**Ask:**
- "How do you remember to do this?" (tracking method)
- "What if you miss this email?" (has it happened?)

---

### Q8. **"When 5 Zoom transcripts dump into Notion at the same time, how do you figure out which is which?"**

**Why ask this:**
- Validates transcript finding (simultaneous dumps)
- Shows sorting/tagging process
- Reveals time cost

**Listen for:**
- How they identify client (by attendees? by time?)
- How often they mis-tag (error rate)
- Time spent on this weekly

**Follow-up:**
- "How often does a transcript just... get lost?" (missed tagging)

---

### Q9. **"If I built a system that automatically pulled tasks from email, Slack, and Zoom into one list - what would you need to see?"**

**Why ask this:**
- Co-design the solution
- Understand their mental model
- Get buy-in on approach

**Listen for:**
- Priority/urgency (how they decide what's first)
- Client context (what info they need)
- Deadlines (do advisors give them?)

**Example answers:**
- "I'd need to see which client it's for"
- "I'd need to know if it's urgent"
- "I'd need the email link so I can see full context"

## SECTION 4: B4B OPERATIONS (Kayla-focused, 10 minutes)

### Goal: Understand B4B intro workflow and monthly reviews

**Say this to Max:**

### Q10. **"Kayla, when a B4B firm emails 'we need a CFO', walk me through what you do - step by step."**

**Why ask this:**
- See full intro workflow
- Identify approval bottleneck
- Understand search process

**Listen for:**
- How long search takes (10 min? 1 hour?)
- How they get advisor approval (email? Slack? call?)
- How they track "already sent to this firm"

**Follow-up questions:**
- "How do you remember if you've already sent Sarah to this firm?" (tracking issue)
- "What if the advisor doesn't respond for 2 days?" (bottleneck)
- "Do you ever send the same profile twice by accident?" (duplicate whispers)

---

### Q11. **"Tell me about the monthly pipeline review - Amanda takes notes, then emails action items. What happens next?"**

**Why ask this:**
- Validate transcript finding (manual process)
- Understand follow-up tracking
- Identify accountability gap

**Listen for:**
- Do people actually do the follow-ups? (compliance)
- How is completion tracked? (or not tracked?)
- How much time between "action assigned" → "action done"

**Follow-up:**
- "At next month's review, how do you know if someone did their follow-up?" (tracking gap)
- "What if someone forgets?" (accountability)

---

### Q12. **"If you could automate ONE thing on the B4B side, what would help you most?"**

**Why ask this:**
- Direct prioritization from her
- Shows what she values
- Validates our hypothesis (probably: monthly review automation or intro tracking)

**Listen for:**
- Emotional response (excitement = high value)
- Specific task name
- Impact statement ("This would save me X hours")

**Why ask this:**

**What to look for:**

**Listen for:**
- Who to contact for access
- Any approval needed
- Timeline (can they give access today? this week?)

## 🚫 WHAT NOT TO DO

### DON'T:
- ❌ Ask about things already answered in transcript (waste of time)
- ❌ Pitch solutions yet ("Here's how we'd fix it...") - JUST LISTEN
- ❌ Promise timelines ("We can build this in 2 weeks") - TOO EARLY
- ❌ Overwhelm with 45 questions - PRIORITIZE
- ❌ Make them feel stupid ("Why don't you just...?") - BE EMPATHETIC
- ❌ Talk about yourself ("I built this before...") - FOCUS ON THEM
- ❌ Take over their screen - LET THEM DRIVE
- ❌ Get technical ("We'll use Make.com with GPT-4...") - SAVE FOR FRIDAY

### DO:
- ✅ Listen 80%, talk 20%
- ✅ Take detailed notes (visible to them - shows you care)
- ✅ Ask "show me" not just "tell me"
- ✅ Validate their pain ("That sounds frustrating")
- ✅ Respect their time (end on time)
- ✅ End with clear next step (access + quick win)
