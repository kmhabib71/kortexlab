## SECTION 2: B4B INTRO REQUEST WORKFLOW (12 minutes) - CORE WORKFLOW

### Q4. **"Walk me through a recent intro request - start to finish. Can you screen-share and show me in Contender?"**

**Why ask:**
- SEE the actual process
- Time each step
- Identify bottlenecks

**What to observe:**
```
✓ How intro request arrives (email? Contender portal? phone?)
✓ How she searches Contender (filters, time)
✓ How she selects matches
✓ How she gets advisor approval
✓ How she tracks completion
```

**Ask DURING screen-share:**
- "How long does this search usually take?"
- "How do you know if you've already sent this exec to this partner?"
- "What if the advisor doesn't respond for 2 days?"

**Time each step:**
- Receive request: ___ (instant or delayed?)
- Search Contender: ___ min
- Get advisor approval: ___ hours/days
- Facilitate intro: ___ min
- Total: ___ min/days

---

### Q5. **"When you search for matches, how do you check 'have I already sent Sarah to this VC firm before'?"**

**Why ask:**
- Validate "no whisper tracking" finding
- Understand current workaround
- Quantify duplicate risk

**Listen for:**
- "I try to remember" (memory-based)
- "I check my email history" (manual search)
- "I just hope I haven't" (no system)
- "I've definitely sent duplicates before" (confirming pain)

**Follow-up:**
- "How often do you accidentally send the same profile twice?"
- "What happens when you do? Are partners annoyed?"

---

### Q6. **"After you send profiles to a B4B partner, how do you track if they responded, requested an intro, or ignored it?"**

**Why ask:**
- Understand outcome tracking
- See if follow-up exists
- Identify reporting gaps

**Listen for:**
- "I don't really track that systematically"
- "If they respond I know, if not I assume they're not interested"
- "I wish I could see if they even viewed the profile"

**Follow-up:**
- "Can you report 'How many whispers led to intros last month'?"
- "How do you know which partners are engaged vs dormant?"

---

### Q7. **"The advisor approval step - David or Liv has to approve your matches before you send, right? How does that work?"**

**Why ask:**
- Identify approval bottleneck
- See if it causes delays
- Understand communication method

**Listen for:**
- "I email them and wait" (bottleneck)
- "Sometimes takes 2 days to hear back" (delay)
- "Sometimes they forget to respond" (dropped)

**Follow-up:**
- "What's the longest you've waited for approval?"
- "Do urgent searches get bottlenecked the same way?"
- "Would it help if you had pre-approved criteria?"

## SECTION 3: MONTHLY PIPELINE REVIEW (10 minutes) - THE TIME SINK

### Q8. **"Tell me about the monthly pipeline review - I heard it's 4-5 hours of manual work. Walk me through what actually happens."**

**Why ask:**
- Validate transcript finding
- Understand full process
- Identify automation opportunities

**Listen for:**
- Zoom call duration (1-2 hours)
- Who attends (David, Amanda, Kayla, others)
- How many deals reviewed (20? 50?)
- Amanda's note-taking process

**Follow-up:**
- "How many deals do you review total?"
- "Is it every single deal or just active ones?"

---

### Q9. **"After the review, Amanda emails action items to everyone - then what? How do you track if those follow-ups actually happen?"**

**Why ask:**
- Understand accountability gap
- See if follow-ups get done
- Identify tracking issues

**Listen for:**
- "I try to do them but sometimes forget"
- "We don't really track completion"
- "Next month we sometimes ask 'did you do X?'"
- "Relies on people remembering"

**Follow-up:**
- "At next month's review, how do you know who did their follow-ups?"
- "What happens if someone doesn't do their assigned task?"

---

### Q10. **"What if instead of a 2-hour monthly Zoom call, you got a weekly email like: 'These 5 deals need attention this week'? Would that work better?"**

**Why ask:**
- Co-design the solution
- Get buy-in on approach
- Validate automation value

**Listen for:**
- Excitement: "That would be amazing"
- Concerns: "But we need to discuss some things as a team"
- Preferences: "Weekly might be too much, bi-weekly?"

**Follow-up:**
- "What would need to be in that email for it to be useful?"
- "Would automated reminders help or just create noise?"

## SECTION 4: TRENDING CAMPAIGNS (5 minutes)
