# Max Meeting Questions - Client Operations

## Monday, Nov 25 - Focus on Executive Client Side

**Duration:** 30-40 minutes
**Max's Role:** Client Ops - Handles exec client onboarding, servicing, narratives, trending
**Goal:** Understand his day-to-day pain and identify top 3 automation opportunities

---

## 🎯 OPENING (2 minutes)

**Say this:**

> "Hey Max! Thanks for making time. I went through the discovery call and was really impressed by what you've built - the Retool dashboards, the way you've organized Notion, all of it. You clearly know systems.
>
> Today I just want to understand your daily reality - not theory, but what actually happens. My goal is to find the automations that'll give you the most time back. Cool?"

---

## SECTION 1: THE BIG PICTURE (5 minutes)

### Q1. **"Can Walk me through your typical day - like what takes up most of your time?"**

**Why ask:**

- See time distribution across tasks
- Identify patterns
- Understand priorities

**Listen for:**

- "Most of my day is [onboarding/narratives/task hunting]"
- Time percentages
- Energy drains vs. energizing work

**Follow-up:**

- "What percentage is 'new' work vs. 'fixing/updating' things?"

---

### Q2. **"If I could magically give you 10 hours back this week, which ONE task would you eliminate completely?"**

**Why ask:**

- Forces prioritization
- Reveals #1 pain point
- Emotional response shows intensity

**Listen for:**

- Immediate answer (means it's top of mind)
- "I would LOVE to never do [X] again"
- Frustration in their voice

**Expected answer:** Probably onboarding or task extraction

---

### Q3. **"What breaks most often? What makes you go 'oh crap, not again'?"**

**Why ask:**

- Reveals brittle processes
- Shows error-prone areas
- Uncovers hidden time sinks

**Listen for:**

- Retool dashboard (missed tagging)
- Whale Sync issues
- Contender profile errors
- Lost Zoom transcripts

---

## SECTION 2: CLIENT ONBOARDING (15 minutes) - THE CORE PAIN

### Q4. **"Can you screen-share and walk me through the last client you onboarded? I want to see the actual process."**

**Why ask:**

- SEE don't just hear
- Identify UI bottlenecks
- Spot hidden steps
- Time each section

**What to observe:**

```
✓ How many tabs open at once?
✓ Copy-paste between systems?
✓ Manual typing vs. dropdowns?
✓ Waiting/loading times?
✓ Have to search for information?
✓ Looking things up elsewhere?
```

**Ask DURING screen-share:**

- "What are you waiting for here?" (delays)
- "Do you ever get this wrong?" (error rate)
- "Where does this information come from?" (data source)

**Time each step:**

- HubSpot: \_\_\_ min
- Notion: \_\_\_ min
- Contender: \_\_\_ min
- Google Drive: \_\_\_ min
- Total: \_\_\_ min

---

### Q5. **"What's the MOST annoying part of onboarding? Is it Contender photo uploads, the tagging, or something else I'm missing?"**

**Why ask:**

- Validate transcript findings
- Find specific UI frustrations
- Might reveal worse pain

**Listen for:**

- "The absolute worst part is..."
- Emotional response
- "I waste so much time on..."

**Expected answers:**

- Contender manual tagging (10 min)
- Photo download/upload (3 min)
- Finding LinkedIn URL (varies)

**Follow-up if Contender:**

- "Show me the Contender tagging - which fields take longest?"
- "How often do you tag something wrong and have to fix it later?"

---

### Q6. **"When TSQ sends you the signed contract PDF, what information is MISSING that you have to hunt for?"**

**Why ask:**

- Reveals data handoff gaps
- Shows where we can pre-fill
- Uncovers communication issues

**Listen for:**

- LinkedIn URL (often missing)
- Client background info
- Have to message advisor
- Billing terms unclear

**Follow-up:**

- "How often do you have to Slack David like 'who is this person?'"
- "Does the contract have everything you need or are you guessing?"

---

### Q7. **"After onboarding, you send the welcome email with kickoff scheduler - does that work smoothly or do clients ghost sometimes?"**

**Why ask:**

- Understand post-onboarding workflow
- See if follow-up automation needed
- Client responsiveness tracking

**Listen for:**

- "Sometimes they don't schedule for weeks"
- Manual follow-ups required
- No tracking of who scheduled vs who didn't

---

## SECTION 3: TASK EXTRACTION CHAOS (10 minutes) - THE TIME BLACK HOLE

### Q8. **"Can Show me your inbox right now - like what does a typical task email from David look like?"**

**Why ask:**

- See actual format
- Understand how tasks arrive
- Identify AI extraction patterns

**What to observe:**

```
✓ Email structure (bullets? paragraphs? chaos?)
✓ How many tasks per email (1 or 10?)
✓ Urgency indicated or implied?
✓ Deadlines mentioned?
✓ Client name clear or buried?
```

**Ask:**

- "How do you track 'I need to do this'? Notion task? Memory? Flag email?"
- "What happens if you miss this email?"
- "Has a task ever gotten lost? What happened?"

---

### Q9. **"Liv and Claire communicate differently than David, right? Can you Show me a Slack task vs an email task."**

**Why ask:**

- Validate "each advisor has different workflow"
- See communication fragmentation
- Understand why tasks get missed

**Listen for:**

- "Liv always uses Slack, David always emails, Claire does both"
- "Sometimes Claire forgets to tell anyone"
- Inconsistency pain

**Follow-up:**

- "Do you check Slack and email separately or is there a system?"
- "How often does something slip through the cracks?"

---

### Q10. **"When 5 Zoom transcripts dump into Notion at the same time, how do you figure out which one belongs to which client?"**

**Why ask:**

- Validate transcript finding (simultaneous dumps)
- Understand sorting process
- Measure time cost

**Listen for:**

- "I look at attendees, but sometimes names don't match"
- "I have to read the first few lines"
- "Sometimes I just guess and fix it later"

**Follow-up:**

- "How long does it take to sort and tag 5 transcripts?"
- "How often do you mis-tag one?"
- "What happens if a transcript never gets tagged?"

---

### Q11. **"suppose Here's a scenario: David emails you 4 tasks, Liv Slacks you 2 tasks, there's a Zoom transcript with Claire mentioning 1 task - how do you handle that?"**

**Why ask:**

- See real prioritization process
- Understand decision-making
- Reveal system gaps

**Listen for:**

- "I usually just do David's first because he's founder"
- "I go by urgency but that's just guessing"
- "I make a quick list in Notion then work through it"

**Follow-up:**

- "How do you decide what's urgent vs what can wait?"
- "Do advisors ever give you deadlines?"

---

## SECTION 4: ONGOING CLIENT WORK (5 minutes)

### Q12. **"After the first 2 months (kickoff, narratives, trending), things get more ad-hoc right? What's the most time-consuming ad-hoc request?"**

**Why ask:**

- Understand Month 3+ chaos
- Identify patterns in "random" requests
- Prioritize automation

**Listen for:**

- P2P introductions (how often?)
- Narrative updates (how long?)
- Additional trending campaigns
- Target mapping

**Follow-up on P2Ps:**

- "How do you track P2P introductions?"
- "Do you ever introduce the same two people twice by accident?"
- "How do you follow up to see if they connected?"

---

### Q13. **"When you do a P2P intro, walk me through it - like how do you find both profiles, draft the email, track it?"**

**Why ask:**

- See specific P2P workflow
- Validate "zero tracking" finding
- Understand intro template process

**Listen for:**

- Time per P2P (15-30 min?)
- Manual email drafting
- No tracking system
- No follow-up

**Follow-up:**

- "Is there any record of P2Ps or is it just 'I emailed them'?"
- "If David asks 'how many P2Ps did we do for Client X?' can you answer?"

---

## SECTION 5: RETOOL DASHBOARD & DATA QUALITY (3 minutes)

### Q14. **"The Retool dashboard breaks when call types aren't tagged, right? How often does that happen?"**

**Why ask:**

- Quantify error rate
- Understand impact
- Validate automation need

**Listen for:**

- Frequency: "All the time" vs "rarely"
- Who makes errors: Max? Advisors?
- Consequences: False alerts? Missed clients?

**Follow-up:**

- "When you realize the dashboard is wrong, how long does it take to fix?"
- "Do you proactively check or only notice when someone points it out?"

---

## SECTION 6: SYSTEMS & ACCESS (3 minutes)

### Q15. **"Can you show me how Whale Sync is set up? What syncs automatically HubSpot → Notion?"**

**Why ask:**

- Understand existing automation
- See what works well
- Identify gaps

**What to observe:**

- Which fields sync automatically
- Which fields you still add manually
- Sync frequency/reliability

---

### Q16. **"Can I get viewer access to HubSpot, Notion, and Retool this week so I can see the setup?"**

**Why ask:**

- Logistics
- Get access early
- Show you're ready to build

**Say this:**

> "I just need to see how everything's connected so I can design automations that fit your workflow. Read-only is totally fine."

**Listen for:**

- Who to contact (you? Amanda? David?)
- Any approval needed
- Timeline (today? this week?)

---

## SECTION 7: QUICK WIN (2 minutes)

### Q17. **"If I built you ONE automation this week as a proof of concept - what would be most valuable to you personally?"**

**Why ask:**

- Get commitment
- Prioritize quick win
- Build excitement

**Listen for:**

- Realistic scope (not "automate everything")
- High personal pain point
- Something we can deliver in 7 days

**Good answers:**

- "Extract tasks from advisor emails automatically" ✅
- "Auto-tag Zoom transcripts with call type" ✅
- "Auto-populate Contender from LinkedIn" ✅

**Bad answers:**

- "Fully automate onboarding" ❌ (needs Contender API)
- "Fix everything" ❌ (too vague)

**Say this:**

> "Great - I'll build that this week and show you on Friday. It won't be perfect but you'll see how it works."

---

### Q18. **"What am I not asking about that I should know?"**

**Why ask:**

- Catch blind spots
- Build trust
- Uncover hidden pain

**Listen for:**

- "Oh, there's also this thing..."
- "The real problem is..."

### Q19. **"What's your biggest worry about automation? Like what's the nightmare scenario?"**

**Why ask:**

- Understand fears
- Address upfront
- Build trust

**Listen for:**

- "What if it breaks and I don't know?"
- "What if I have to learn a complicated new system?"
- "What if it makes more work somehow?"

>
> - We'll build with error alerts so you KNOW if something breaks
> - We'll keep it simple - mostly background automation
> - You'll see the quick win this week before we build anything big"

## 🚫 DON'T DO THIS

- ❌ Ask about things already in transcript (waste his time)
- ❌ Pitch solutions yet ("Here's how we'll fix it")
- ❌ Promise timelines ("We'll have this done in 2 weeks")
- ❌ Make him feel dumb ("Why don't you just...")
- ❌ Go over 40 minutes (respect his time)

## ✅ DO THIS

- ✅ Listen 80%, talk 20%
- ✅ Take visible notes (shows you care)
- ✅ Ask to SEE things, not just hear about them
- ✅ Validate his pain ("That sounds incredibly frustrating")
- ✅ End with clear action (quick win + access request)
