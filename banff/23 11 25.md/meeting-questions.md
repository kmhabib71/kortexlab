## Question Strategy & Tips

### DO:
- ✅ **Listen more than you talk** (80/20 rule)
- ✅ **Take detailed notes** (create new .md files for each meeting)
- ✅ **Ask follow-up questions** when something is unclear
- ✅ **Reference the transcript** to show you did your homework
- ✅ **Validate your assumptions** ("I heard X in the transcript, is that accurate?")
- ✅ **Ask for examples** ("Can you show me in Notion what this looks like?")
- ✅ **Respect their time** (prioritize top questions if running short)

### DON'T:
- ❌ **Don't pitch solutions yet** (this is discovery, not sales)
- ❌ **Don't say "that's easy to automate"** (minimizes their pain)
- ❌ **Don't talk about yourself** (focus on their workflows)
- ❌ **Don't promise timelines** (you're still learning their systems)
- ❌ **Don't interrupt** (let them finish thoughts)
